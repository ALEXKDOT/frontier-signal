# Frontier Signal

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Medical doctors, MD/PhDs, and other highly educated professionals who may be unfamiliar with AI. They need substantive, concise explanations, clear evidence limitations, and an accessible account of the current model landscape.

## Product Purpose

A public, independently updated compendium of frontier AI developments, material risk events, and effective safeguards. Readers can identify currently offered models, understand major incidents, and examine the evidence behind a qualitative five-year outlook.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- GitHub Pages at https://alexkdot.github.io/frontier-signal/, maintained from ALEXKDOT/frontier-signal. Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; `dist/` is the deployed application.
- Five distinct views: Dashboard (default), Overview, Timeline, Concern ladder, Methodology.
- Dashboard: five-provider selector, two featured models per provider (most capable and everyday use), expandable full catalog, and evidence-linked major incidents.
- Model cards use documented release dates, precise purpose descriptions, availability, and sources. Restricted access is red and legible.
- Simplified vocabulary is off by default and opt-in.
- Source checks run weekly on Mondays. Model and incident freshness are separate; the interface must never imply continuous or real-time ingestion.
- Preserve historical import provenance and distinguish it from automated, source-checked summaries. Do not invent human approval, benchmark comparisons, risk probabilities, or evidence.
- Research updates follow editorial/WEEKLY_UPDATE.md. The user approved automatic publication to the existing GitHub site.

## Brand Commitments

Frontier Signal / frontiersignal. Concise, professional, evidence-led language; no promotional UX filler or condescending explanations. The user granted creative freedom to redesign the interface and requested assistance from pbakaus/impeccable.

## Evidence on Hand

`dist/data/signals.json`, `dist/data/models.json`, and `dist/data/glossary.json` contain the established research, model catalog, and definitions. Existing editorial records document sourcing and uncertainty. Their findings are content to preserve during a visual redesign.

## Product Principles

- Separate observations, interpretations, and forecasts.
- Give safeguards and counterevidence equal analytical attention.
- Prefer specific, attributable information over generic labels.
- Support both a quick orientation and deeper source inspection.

Captured from the user's prior requests and the existing application on October 5, 2026; no new product claims were inferred.
